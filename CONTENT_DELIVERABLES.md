## 2. Technical Article / Blog Post

### Title: **Never Forget a Client Constraint: Building a Persistent Sales Memory Co-Pilot with Hindsight**

#### The Problem: Stateless AI in High-Stakes Negotiations
In B2B sales and high-ticket services, customer context is everything. Standard chatbots are stateless—they treat every interaction as Day 1. When drafting a proposal, they offer generic packages and ignore constraints the buyer shared weeks prior.

#### The DealAnchor Solution
DealAnchor integrates **Vectorize Hindsight** as an intelligent memory bank.
1. **Continuous Retention (`retain`):** Automatically captures customer objections, budget boundaries, and competitor feedback after every call.
2. **Context-Informed Drafting (`recall`):** When generating a proposal, DealAnchor recalls prior notes to produce tailored commercial offers that directly resolve past objections.
3. **Cross-Deal Reflection (`reflect`):** Discovers company-wide sales trends (e.g., that 67% of prospects drop off when proposals exceed their Meeting 1 budget cap).
